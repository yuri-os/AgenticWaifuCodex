# E — Communities and Where to Hang Out

The active communities for this niche, in 2026, with what they're good for.

## RP / character cards

- **SillyTavern Discord.** Power-user technical RP community. Card craft, prompts, frontend.
- **Chub.ai Discord.** Marketplace + creators. Distribution + collaboration.
- **JanitorAI subreddit + Discord.** Mass-market RP audience.
- **r/SillyTavernAI.** Technical RP discussion.

## AI companion / waifu

- **r/AICompanions, r/AIRomance.** Mainstream audience.
- **r/MyBoyfriendIsAI.** Vocal user community — useful intelligence on what users actually want.
- **AICharacterCards Discord.** Card-creator-oriented.

## VTuber / virtual avatars

- **r/VirtualYoutubers.** General VTuber audience.
- **r/AIVtuber.** AI-driven specifically.
- **VRoid Hub / Pixiv VRM communities.** Avatar art + distribution.
- **VRChat community.** Avatar distribution as marketing.

## AI builder / open-source

- **HuggingFace Discord + forums.** Models, adapters, datasets.
- **LocalLLaMA on Reddit.** Local LLM news + tooling.
- **r/LocalLLaMA, r/MachineLearning.** Mainstream.
- **The Pile of LLM Discord servers** (TheBloke, vLLM, etc.). Niche but knowledgeable.

## Indie builders / creator economy

- **Indie Hackers.** Build-in-public + monetisation.
- **Hacker News.** Distribution + technical credibility.
- **r/SaaS, r/SideProject.** Builder audience.
- **Microconf.** Conference + community for bootstrapped SaaS.

## Anime / cyberpunk aesthetic

- **r/Cyberpunk, r/animenocontext, r/AnimeArt.** Aesthetic alignment.
- **/cyb/ adjacent forums** — selectively. Tonally aligned audience exists.

## Etiquette notes

- Read before you post. Each community has tonal rules.
- Don't drop links cold; build credibility first.
- Don't crosspost the same content to all of them.
- The waifu/companion subreddit communities are often emotionally raw — read posts seriously before replying.
